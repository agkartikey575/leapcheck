Hidden libraries inside WordPress plugins

Plugins often ship their own copies of other libraries. Old copies are a common reason sites break during PHP upgrades, and some carry known vulnerabilities. We found 796 different libraries bundled in the latest scanned version of 1000 plugins; 25 of them have at least one copy with known vulnerabilities.
