# New idea scan: deadline-driven needs (2026-11-01)

Test applied: urgent deadline, legal to serve, low dependence on outside companies, and an empty marketplace confirmed by a live check.

| Need | Deadline | Live competition check | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EU AI Act Art. 50:** chatbot disclosure and labelling of AI content (C2PA) | In force since 2 Aug 2026; machine-readable marking grace period ends 2 Dec 2026 | WordPress.org: "EU AI Act Ready", "SEO'n'AI Image check&mark", "Image AI Labels", "Enfants IMG Performer", "TransparAI", "EU AI Label". All have ≤100 installs. C2PA signing APIs: Trufo ($120/mo+), Capture ($0.001/call), SSL.com free certificate | Crowded with tiny tools. Low installs suggest weak demand from website owners (most duties fall on AI *providers*). C2PA signing needs certificates from approved third-party authorities, which adds outside dependency |
| **EU Cyber Resilience Act:** SBOM, vulnerability handling, 24h reporting | Reporting live since 11 Sep 2026; full obligations 11 Dec 2027 | WordPress.org: 7 CRA plugins (≤50 installs). GitHub: fendkit, @ambolt/cra, cra-compliance-kit, others. SaaS: Venvera €399/mo+ | Crowded with free open-source tools; no clear winner yet |
| **EUDR (deforestation):** due-diligence statements | 30 Dec 2026 large/medium; 30 Jun 2027 micro/small | osapiens, Coolset, Tracklab, Complir, Clearlane, EUDR Navigator, Polygon One… | Crowded, enterprise-heavy, legally complex |
| **US app-store age laws** (TX, UT, LA, CA) | Dates moving; Texas in litigation; California 1 Jan 2027 | Apple/Google ship the official APIs | Unstable law, high legal risk, platform-owned |

## Lesson
In 2026, AI makes building cheap, so **every regulation that makes the news is flooded with small tools within weeks.** The products we found have very few installs, which tells us building the tool is not the bottleneck. The edge has to come from finding needs that *aren't* in the news, plus distribution and trust.

## Proposed method change: "pain mining"
Mine real, repeated, unsolved complaints instead of reading regulation news:
- 1–3★ reviews and support threads of high-install WordPress/Shopify/Chrome products
- "Is there a plugin/app that…" posts on Reddit and forums
- GitHub issues with many 👍 and "won't fix" labels
Pick needs with many requests, money involved, and no good existing fix. Then verify again with a live marketplace check.
